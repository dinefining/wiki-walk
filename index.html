wiki park

A homage to a time when discovering things meant stumbling upon them. Conversations, tidbits of trivia, links that led somewhere unexpected.

Wiki Park is a shared landscape of Wikipedia articles at the intersection of nature, technology and being human. There's no search, no zoom, no map. Take a walk, see what's around you, stop when something catches your eye. Leave a lantern if you'd like to find your way back.

There's no need to get anywhere.

  • Scroll, drag or use arrow keys to wander.
  • Click a plant to read.
  • Press L or tap a card's lantern to leave one for others. Press again to take it back. Three per day. The corner pointer leads to the nearest lantern.
  • Double-click open ground to plant an article. One per day.
  • It's always night here. Only your lantern lights the way.
  • Pale arrows are other walkers. Tiny dots mark plants someone has planted.
